Affiliate disclosure

Last updated September 2026.

How Shelfwise makes money

Shelfwise participates in affiliate programs, including the Amazon Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by linking to Amazon.com and affiliated sites. We're also in the process of applying to additional affiliate programs for other products we cover, such as online courses.

When you click certain links on this site and go on to make a purchase or sign up for a paid product, we may earn a commission. This comes at no additional cost to you — the price you pay is the same whether you use our link or go directly to the merchant.

How this affects what we recommend

Being paid a commission never determines which products we choose to review, or what we say about them. We write our honest assessment of a product first, based on its actual features, pricing, and how well it does what it claims — whether or not we currently have an affiliate relationship with it. Several reviews on this site currently link directly to the merchant with no commission involved at all, simply because we haven't been approved into that specific program yet; those pages say so explicitly.

Which links are affiliate links

Not every outbound link on Shelfwise is an affiliate link. Where a commission relationship exists, the relevant review page says so directly, near the link itself — we don't hide this in fine print at the bottom of the page. If a review currently has no affiliate relationship in place, we say that too, rather than implying one that doesn't exist.

Questions

If you have any questions about this disclosure or how we cover a specific product, feel free to reach out through our contact page.
