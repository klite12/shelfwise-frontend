Quick summary

Data Analytics with Python is a 40-hour, self-paced course built around real datasets rather than toy exercises. It covers Python fundamentals, pandas for data wrangling, SQL for querying, and dashboarding with a lightweight BI tool. The course ends with a capstone project you can add straight to a portfolio, and a certificate that several hiring partners recognize.

Who it's for

Good fit

  • Career-changers targeting analyst roles
  • People comfortable with spreadsheets already
  • Learners who want a portfolio piece, not just a certificate

Not a fit

  • Complete beginners to computers or logic
  • People wanting live, instructor-led classes
  • Anyone needing a payment plan under $50/mo

Features

  • 40 hours of project-based video lessons
  • Real, de-identified datasets across five industries
  • Capstone project with portfolio review
  • Shareable certificate on completion
  • Private community forum for cohort learners

Pricing

The course is a one-time $129 payment with lifetime access to updates, or $19/month for those who prefer to spread the cost over roughly seven months. There's no free trial, but a 14-day refund window covers most of the first module.

Pros and cons

Pros
  • Real datasets instead of toy examples
  • Active, well-moderated community
  • Certificate recognized by several hiring partners
  • Clear capstone deliverable for portfolios
Cons
  • Pace moves quickly for total beginners

Alternatives to consider

If you want more hand-holding, a bootcamp-style program with live cohorts and mentorship may suit you better, at a higher price point. Frequently asked questions

Do I need any coding experience before starting?

No prior coding experience is required, but comfort with spreadsheet formulas will make the first two weeks easier.

Is the certificate accredited?

It's not a university accreditation — it's an industry-recognized completion certificate that several listed hiring partners accept as a portfolio credential.

How long do I have access to the material?

The one-time payment plan includes lifetime access, including future content updates. The monthly plan gives access for as long as the subscription is active.

Is there a refund policy?

Yes — a 14-day refund window applies from the enrollment date, covering roughly the first module of content.

Final verdict

A strong pick for career-changers who want a portfolio, not just a certificate

Data Analytics with Python earns its 4.8 rating on the strength of its project-based structure and active community. It isn't the cheapest or gentlest option on the market, but for learners who are ready to move quickly, it's one of the more practical analytics courses we've reviewed.
