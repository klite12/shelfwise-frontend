How we work

What "reviewed honestly" actually means here

No listing gets added just because it pays a commission. Every product on Shelfwise is checked against the same short list of standards before it's published.

What earns a spot

  • Clear, specific pros and cons — not just marketing copy
  • A real price, refund policy, and what you actually get
  • Enough substance to be worth a dedicated review page

What gets left out

  • Products we can't verify pricing or features for
  • Listings with no real differentiation from cheaper alternatives
  • Anything we wouldn't personally recommend to a friend
